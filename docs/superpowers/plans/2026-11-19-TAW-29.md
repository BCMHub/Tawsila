# TAW-29 Post and discover rides on one route: implementation plan (scope revision 2)

> **For agentic workers:** execute task by task in the TAW-29 implementation worktree, tests first (Critical lane: full TDD). REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:subagent-driven-development or superpowers:executing-plans. Track steps with the checkboxes in section 6.

**Goal:** A phone-verified driver with an approved car posts a ride (once or every weekday) on the configured pilot route. Signed-in members list future rides with free seats by direction and day, open one, and share a WhatsApp link that opens the ride in the installed app with its current state.

**Spec (binding):** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-19-TAW-29-design.md`, gated blob `eb329e40e75d7b447a03a93d416207c3cec134ee` (spec gate PASS, round 6, commit `41dfb1d`). The spec governs wherever this plan is silent. Its section 5 (function contracts and error codes), section 8.1 (lock order and concurrency), section 13 (test list) and section 14 (expected-privilege table and per-function outcomes) are **binding test sources**: every bullet there becomes at least one assertion.

**Criteria:** TAW-29 scope revision 2, AC1–AC8, snapshot sha256 `5990b1c27448ed74ec4f5e8b51005ab1e09881fe4c8b4416e62625096313ab0f`.

**Design:** Figma `2j3vO1USLBA8XiRpQKCF3H`, MVP page `228:807`, sections `228:808` and `228:809` (P1a–P3, D1–D4b, light and dark; approved under TAW-28), with the eight recorded deviations of spec section 11.2.

**Architecture:** Three migrations add configuration tables (places, routes, `car_types.passenger_seats`), ride tables (`ride_series`, `rides` with one row per day) and `pilot_events`. Client roles have no table privileges, so every user action goes through `security definer` RPCs, and publishing is gated on `require_phone_verified()` and `can_post_rides()`. A `pg_cron` job keeps 14 days of weekday rides. The Expo app adds a rides feature module, post and detail routes, a share-link route and a native-intent hook for links.

**Tech stack:** Supabase (Postgres 17, pgTAP, `pg_cron`), Expo SDK 58 with expo-router, TypeScript, Jest (jest-expo), local Supabase stack (Docker/Colima).

**Lane reassessment (authoring-workflow section 1, before the plan gate, 2026-11-19):** LANE: Critical — new ride tables and RLS, server-side publish authorisation, a scheduled job, multi-screen feature; unchanged. No new Critical trigger was found during authoring. The weekday extension job and the universal link are within the Critical classification already confirmed by the owner.

- TAW-4 and TAW-7 migrations, functions and tests are not modified. TAW-29 only **adds** migrations; the one change to a TAW-4 table is the new `car_types.passenger_seats` column.
