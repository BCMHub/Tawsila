# TAW-30 Request a seat; driver accepts or declines: implementation plan (scope revision 4)

> **For agentic workers:** execute task by task in the TAW-30 implementation worktree, tests first (Critical lane: full TDD). REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:subagent-driven-development or superpowers:executing-plans. Track steps with the checkboxes in section 6.

**Goal:** A phone-verified rider with a completed profile requests seats on one ride day; the driver accepts or declines; the server allocates seats without overbooking; riders and drivers cancel with actor, time and reason; pending requests expire; both sides see status in the app (My trips, the ride, the request).

**Spec (binding):** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-21-TAW-30-design.md`, blob `00988b401a9b9b6f6b88eb6d4821fbc528474e72` (commit `d40c0ee`). Spec gate: 21 rounds, last block FAIL with two open P1s that are test-design items (F104 scenario 31, F107 pause before the pending count), repaired in this blob and not gate-reviewed; the owner ended the spec rounds on 2026-11-21 and directed that the remaining items be fixed and carried into this plan. This plan's gate reviews this blob together with the plan; the owner's acceptance of the spec-gate outcome against TAW-37 AC2 is recorded at document approval. The spec governs wherever this plan is silent. Its sections 3 (data model), 4 (time and states), 5 (function contracts and error codes), 8 (lock order, scenarios 1–31, the job, expiry and scheduler evidence), 13 (test list, mutation and regression evidence) and 14 (expected-privilege rows and per-function outcomes) are **binding test sources**: every bullet there becomes at least one assertion, scenario or Jest case.

**Criteria:** TAW-30 scope revision 4, AC1–AC10, snapshot sha256 `9f4feee4031dbfd9ac5ec479c37199ea5fe7b50cffe7ddc9de54ac410f4a54ac`.

**Design:** Figma `2j3vO1USLBA8XiRpQKCF3H`, MVP page `228:807`, section `228:810` (R1–R9f, light and dark; approved under TAW-28), with the 20 recorded deviations of spec section 11.2 (shown to the owner at document approval). R9c and R9d are not built (TAW-40).

**Architecture:** One schema migration adds `seat_requests`, `seat_holds` and `cancellations` (RLS on, no policies, no API-role privileges), changes `pilot_events` (checks, six columns, nullable `user_id`, `service_role` `SELECT` revoked, sequence revoke re-asserted), revokes `service_role` writes on `accounts` (O5) and adds the future-`cancelled_at` guard trigger on `rides` and `ride_series`. One function migration adds the `security definer` client functions and owner-only helpers of spec 5, replaces `taw29_new_share_code` and `extend_weekday_series` (locking only) and schedules `expire_seat_requests`. The Expo app adds a `requests` feature module, R1–R9 screens, the Trips tab and status-driven D2 and shared-view rendering.

**Tech stack:** Supabase (Postgres 17, pgTAP, `pg_cron`), Expo SDK 58 with expo-router, TypeScript, Jest (jest-expo), local Supabase stack (Docker/Colima).

**Lane reassessment (authoring-workflow section 1, before the plan gate, 2026-11-21):** LANE: Critical — new seat tables and RLS, server-side request authorisation, seat allocation under concurrency, a new scheduled job and a replaced one, a grant tightening on `accounts`, `pilot_events` and the TAW-29 job, a multi-screen feature; unchanged.
